How Our Fares Work

What exactly am I paying for?

The distance you travel, multiplied by the per-KM rate for your vehicle. Nothing else is built into that number. Toll, parking and inter-state permit charges are passed on at cost and shown separately, so you can always see what went where.

Why is the outstation rate lower than the local rate?

Outstation trips cover long, steady highway distance, so the running cost per kilometre is lower than stop-start city driving. We pass that difference on rather than charging a flat rate for both.

Does the distance include the return journey?

Yes. Every outstation trip is a round trip — the vehicle stays with you and comes back with you — so the distance you enter should be the full journey out and back. For local hires, the kilometres included in your hourly package cover the whole day. We confirm the chargeable distance with you before the trip starts.

Is this figure the final bill?

It is an estimate based on the distance you enter. The final fare is worked out on the actual distance run, at the same per-KM rate you were quoted. If your plans change mid-trip and you cover more ground, the extra is charged at that same rate — never a higher one.

What is the difference between a local hire and an outstation trip?

A local hire stays within Bengaluru city and is sold as an hourly package — 4, 8 or 12 hours, each with kilometres included. An outstation trip leaves the city, is always a round trip with the vehicle staying with you, and is billed on distance plus a daily minimum and the driver’s allowance.

What is the minimum distance charged per day?

An outstation vehicle is committed to you for the whole day, so each day of the hire bills a floor of 250 KM for cars and 300 KM for the minibus. Cover more than that and you simply pay for the actual distance. A two-day trip covering 350 KM is therefore billed on the floor, not on 350 KM — the calculator above shows you exactly when this applies.

What is driver bata, and why is it separate?

Bata is the driver’s daily allowance for food and rest on an outstation trip — ₹300 per day, with a further ₹300 for any night the driver is running between 10 PM and 6 AM. We show it as its own line rather than burying it in the per-KM rate, so you can see what the rate covers and what the driver receives.

Are there night charges or waiting charges?

No night surcharge — a 5 AM airport drop costs the same per kilometre as a midday trip. Extended waiting outside the agreed pickup window may be charged, and we will tell you before it applies rather than adding it to the bill afterwards.

State Permit Charges

What changes when your trip crosses a state border.

A commercial vehicle registered in Karnataka needs a state entry permit to carry passengers into another state. The permit is bought from that state’s transport department for the duration of your trip, and it is charged to you at cost — we do not mark it up.

No permit needed

Anywhere within Karnataka — Mysuru, Coorg, Chikkamagaluru, Hampi, Dharmasthala, Mangaluru and everything in between.

Permit applies

Tamil Nadu (Ooty, Chennai), Kerala (Wayanad, Munnar), Andhra Pradesh (Tirupati), Telangana (Hyderabad), Puducherry and Goa.

How we handle it

  • We tell you at the time of quoting whether your route needs a permit.
  • The driver arranges it — you do not need to visit any office.
  • The exact amount depends on the state, the vehicle’s seating capacity and the number of days, so it is confirmed rather than estimated.
  • The receipt is handed to you with the final bill.
Permit, toll and parking sit outside the fare shown by the calculator. That keeps the per-KM rate honest — you are never paying a padded rate to cover charges your particular route may not attract.
