Common Questions

How is the fare worked out?

Distance in kilometres × the per-KM rate for your vehicle. Outstation trips also carry a minimum chargeable distance per day and the driver’s allowance, both shown as separate lines. Toll, parking and state permit are extra at cost. The fare calculator shows the whole build-up before you commit to anything.

How far ahead should I book?

A day is comfortable for city trips and airport runs. For outstation journeys, the minibus, or anything in wedding season, give us two to three days so we can put the right vehicle and driver on it.

Do you cover trips outside Karnataka?

Yes — Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana are regular runs for us. Those routes need a state entry permit, which we arrange and pass on at cost. We tell you at the quoting stage whether your route needs one.

How do I pay?

Cash, UPI or bank transfer at the end of the trip. Longer outstation trips may need an advance towards fuel and driver expenses, and corporate clients can be invoiced monthly.
