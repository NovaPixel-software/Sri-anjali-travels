Frequently Asked

How is the fare calculated?

Fare = total distance in kilometres × the per-KM rate for your vehicle. Local and outstation have separate rates, and both are published on the vehicles page. Toll, parking and state permit charges are extra where they apply.

Do you charge for the return distance on outstation trips?

For a one-way drop, the vehicle still has to come back, so the quoted distance includes the return leg. We tell you the total kilometres being charged before you confirm — there is never a surprise on the final bill.

How far in advance should I book?

A day ahead is comfortable for local trips and airport runs. For outstation journeys, minibuses or wedding season, give us two to three days so we can assign the right vehicle and driver.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us as early as you can. Changes made well before the pickup time carry no charge; last-minute cancellations after a vehicle has been dispatched may attract a small fee to cover the driver's running.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI and bank transfer. Corporate clients can be set up on a monthly account with a consolidated invoice — email us to arrange it.

Are night trips available?

Yes, bookings are taken around the clock and we run early-morning airport drops daily. For long night drives we assign a relief driver rather than push a single driver past safe hours.
