Terms & Conditions

The ground rules for every trip we run.

Last updated: 28 August 2026

1. Bookings

  • An enquiry sent through this website is a request, not a confirmed booking. Your trip is confirmed only once our team calls or messages you back.
  • Each confirmed enquiry gets a reference number in the format SATyymmddnn. Quote it when you contact us about that trip.
  • Vehicles are assigned by type, not by registration number. We may substitute an equivalent or better vehicle if the assigned one becomes unavailable.

2. Fares

  • Fares are calculated as distance in kilometres × the per-KM rate for the chosen vehicle. Local and outstation carry different rates.
  • The figure shown by the fare calculator is an estimate based on the distance you enter. The final fare is based on the actual distance run.
  • Toll charges, parking fees, inter-state permits and entry taxes are extra and billed at cost.
  • Outstation trips are round trips only. The vehicle remains with you for the duration of the hire and returns with you, so the quoted distance covers the full journey out and back. The total kilometres being charged are confirmed to you before the trip.
  • Local hires are within Bengaluru city limits and are sold as hourly packages (4, 8 or 12 hours), each including a set number of kilometres. Kilometres beyond the package are charged at the same per-KM rate, and each additional hour at the published hourly rate.
  • Waiting beyond the agreed pickup window, and any additional running not in the original plan, may be charged separately at the same per-KM rate.
  • Published rates may change. The rate confirmed to you at the time of booking is the rate that applies to your trip.

3. Payment

  • We accept cash, UPI and bank transfer. Payment is due at the end of the trip unless agreed otherwise in advance.
  • Multi-day outstation trips may require an advance towards fuel and driver expenses.
  • Corporate accounts can be invoiced monthly by prior arrangement.

4. Cancellations and changes

  • Tell us as early as you can. Changes made well before the pickup time are free.
  • If a vehicle has already been dispatched towards your pickup, a cancellation fee may apply to cover the driver's running.
  • We may cancel a trip in cases of vehicle breakdown, driver illness, unsafe road or weather conditions, or events outside our control. In such cases we will offer a replacement vehicle where possible, or refund any advance paid.

5. During the journey

  • The driver decides the route based on road conditions and safety. Tell us in advance if you need a specific route or stops.
  • Driving hours are limited for safety. Long night journeys are planned with a relief driver or a rest break rather than pushing a single driver.
  • Smoking, alcohol and any illegal substance are not permitted in our vehicles.
  • Passengers are responsible for their own belongings. Please check the vehicle before you get out — we will help trace anything left behind, but we cannot accept liability for it.
  • Any damage to the vehicle caused by a passenger, including soiling that needs professional cleaning, will be charged at cost.

6. Passengers and luggage

Seating and luggage capacities listed for each vehicle are limits, not suggestions. We cannot carry more passengers than the vehicle is registered for, and excess luggage may require a larger vehicle at the applicable rate.

7. Liability

All vehicles carry valid registration, fitness, permit and insurance as required by law. Our liability is limited to the service we provide and the cover carried by that insurance. We are not liable for indirect losses such as missed flights, missed connections or consequential costs arising from delays caused by traffic, weather, road closures or other circumstances beyond our control.

8. This website

The admin panel on this site is a demonstration tool. Rates and enquiries shown in it are stored in the browser being used and are not a system of record. In case of any difference, the fare and details confirmed to you directly by our team apply.

9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and any dispute falls under the jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka.
