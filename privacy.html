Privacy Policy

How we handle the details you share with us.

Last updated: 28 August 2026

What we collect

When you send a booking enquiry or contact us, we collect only what we need to arrange your trip:

  • Your name and mobile number, so we can confirm the booking and the driver can reach you
  • Your email address, if you choose to give one
  • Trip details — vehicle type, travel date, pickup and drop, approximate distance
  • Any notes you add, such as luggage or accessibility requirements

We do not ask for and never store payment card details, bank credentials, or identity document numbers through this website.

How we use it

  • To confirm your booking and quote the fare
  • To share the assigned driver's name and number with you before pickup
  • To contact you about that trip if plans change
  • To keep a record of completed trips for our own accounts

We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone. We do not send marketing messages unless you have asked us to.

Who sees it

Your name, number and pickup details are shared with the driver assigned to your trip, because they need them to complete the journey. Nobody else outside our team receives your details, except where we are required to share them by law or by a lawful request from a government authority.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and trip records are kept for as long as we need them for our accounts and for any follow-up you may raise. You can ask us to delete your details at any time and we will do so, except where we are required to retain a record for tax or legal reasons.

This website

This site does not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics. It loads a webfont from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers receive the request for that font file. Anything you type into a form on this site stays in your own browser until you choose to send it to us over WhatsApp, email or a phone call.

Your choices

  • Ask us what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your details
  • Ask us to stop contacting you

To make any of these requests, call or WhatsApp +91 12345 67890. We will respond within seven working days.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be mentioned when you next make a booking.
