Who We Are

A travel partner your family can trust

Sri Anjali Travels is a Bengaluru-based travel company committed to providing safe, comfortable and hassle-free journeys. We believe in transparent fares, well maintained vehicles and customer satisfaction above all else.

What began as a single car serving families around Bengaluru has grown into a fleet covering city runs, airport transfers and long outstation routes across South India. The promise has not changed: you know the fare before you travel, the vehicle turns up clean and on time, and a real person answers the phone if anything changes.

Most of our work still comes from repeat customers and their referrals — which is exactly how we would like to keep growing.

What We Stand For

Transparent Pricing

One published rate per kilometre. Toll, parking and state permit are called out separately — never slipped in at the end.

Safety First

Serviced vehicles, valid papers, licensed drivers and no night-driving beyond safe hours without a relief driver.

Punctuality

Airport and station pickups are planned backwards from your time, with buffer for traffic. Early beats late.

Local Knowledge

Our drivers know the ghat roads, the temple timings and where to stop for a decent meal along the way.
